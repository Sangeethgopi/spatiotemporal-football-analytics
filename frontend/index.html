Spatiotemporal Football Analytics Framework

A temporally causal, data-driven framework for estimating and detecting turnover-associated pressing dynamics from football tracking data and computer vision. The platform transforms player movement and event context into interpretable analytical signals for studying pressing behaviour around possession turnovers.
